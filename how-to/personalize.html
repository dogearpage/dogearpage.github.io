Personalize

Pick a theme, fonts and display options. Takes about a minute.

Open Settings

Click Settings at the bottom of the Bookmarks page. Every change applies the moment you make it, so you can try things and watch the page behind the window. Click Close when you're happy.

Theme and fonts are shared: set them on either the Bookmarks or the Acronyms page and both pages (and these help pages) follow. Like your links, settings are saved in this browser only.

Icon style

Controls the icon beside each category name:

  • Colorful: a two-tone icon on a white chip, tinted with the category's color.
  • Simple: a plain white icon.
  • Emoji: the emoji you gave the category instead of an icon.
  • None: names only.

Each category's icon, emoji and color are set when you edit the category.

Fonts

There are two dropdowns, and each option is shown in its own typeface so you can preview it (in Firefox; other browsers show the chosen font once you pick it).

  • Heading font is used for the page title and category names (table headers on the Acronyms page). It includes playful display fonts such as Bebas Neue, Permanent Marker, Creepster and Mountains of Christmas.
  • Body font is used for everything else. Display fonts are left out of this list because they're hard to read in long text.

Tip: the holiday themes pair well with a matching heading font, like Halloween with Creepster or Christmas with Mountains of Christmas.

Display options

SettingWhat it does
Bookmark descriptionsInline puts the description beside the link name, Below the name gives it its own line, None hides descriptions for a compact list.
Show site faviconsShows each site's little logo next to its link. Off by default because the favicons come from Google's favicon service, which means each bookmark's domain is sent to Google.

Show date & timeShows or hides the clock line under the title.
Header textThe greeting itself (starts as "Hi how are you?"). It's also the name shown on the browser tab.
Date & time formatHow the clock line is written. Leave it blank to hide it. A live preview appears under the box.

The format uses short codes. The most useful ones:

CodeGivesCodeGives
dddd / dddMonday / Monh / hh / HH2 / 02 / 14 (hour)
MMMM / MMM / MNovember / Nov / 11mm05 (minutes)
D / DD9 / 09 (day)A / aPM / pm
YYYY / YY2026 / 26ss09 (seconds)

Put ordinary words in square brackets so they aren't read as codes. Examples:

  • dddd, MMMM D, YYYY h:mm A → Thursday, November 19, 2026 6:20 PM (the default)
  • ddd MMM D [at] h:mm a → Thu Nov 19 at 6:20 pm
  • YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm → 2026-11-19 18:20

Changed your mind?

Just pick different options; nothing is permanent. Your settings are included when you Export JSON, so restoring a backup brings your look back too.
